# CmdTab: 1,000 waitlist signups in 30 days

Prepared 9 October 2026. Campaign dates: **9 October–7 November**, Asia/Singapore; final report on **8 November**. Status: **draft assets; no distribution or spend executed**. Launch is conditional on the updated waitlist website passing production checks and being deployed. Dates are a proposed operating schedule, not evidence of publication.

The target is **1,000 net-new, unique, durably stored waitlist email addresses**. Count normalized addresses stored after the campaign start, excluding existing entries, repeat submissions, internal/test records and identified spam. This is a waitlist acquisition target, not website viewers or active app users. An address is not a verified person or verified inbox without an actual confirmation mechanism. No outbound email is authorized in this campaign.

The original `one-month-1000-active-users-plan.md` and campaign kit remain preserved in the original checkout. This new campaign updates their assumptions: no verified-address target, email marketing, or paid distribution. Historical traffic snapshots are not a current baseline. Record fresh aggregate visitor and unique stored signup counts before launch; without them, growth versus baseline is **unknown**.

Fresh baseline supplied by the coordinating agent on 9 October: production Vercel aggregate for 9 September 00:00–9 October 00:00 Asia/Singapore returned **15 visitors and 33 pageviews**. The provider rounded the response boundary to 8 October 17:00 UTC; retain the original query/response with qualification evidence. Provider visitor counts are estimates with their own coverage, not proof of unique humans. The 12,500-visit planning scenario is about **833 times** this measured monthly visitor baseline, before considering visitor-versus-visit coverage differences. Existing waitlist count and signup conversion remain unverified. This scale requires substantial audience distribution and cannot be guaranteed.

## Positioning and audience

**Find the window. Get back to work.** CmdTab is a macOS window switcher in private preview. The website currently accepts waitlist requests; public downloads, paid purchases, and trials are unavailable. Do not promise a release date, time saved, customer count, stability certification, or endorsement.

Start with Mac developers, designers, analysts, and operators who keep several windows open. Lead with a recognizable problem: Command-Tab selects an application when the user is trying to find a specific window. Teach the built-in shortcuts first and explain where CmdTab's intended workflow differs. Use the site's actual feature and permission descriptions; claims must match the deployed candidate. Use real capture only after verification, otherwise clearly label the existing showcase as a product illustration.

## Acquisition budget and decision points

These are **effort allocations and stretch targets, not forecasts**. Audiences, click rates, partner participation and signup conversion have not been measured. With an assumed **8% new unique stored-signup conversion**, 1,000 signups requires **12,500 qualified website visits** (about 417 per day). This is a large distribution requirement; SEO alone cannot credibly deliver it on this timetable. No paid acquisition or email outreach is included.

| Channel | Qualified visits target | New stored signups at assumed 8% | Distribution hypothesis |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Permissioned Mac communities | 4,500 | 360 | Nine independently permitted useful posts averaging 500 visits; actual community size and click rates unverified |
| Creators/newsletters | 4,000 | 320 | Four independently agreed shares averaging 1,000 visits; ten tailored proposal drafts, no messages sent |
| Founder LinkedIn/X/Threads | 2,500 | 200 | Ten substantive posts averaging 250 visits; owner audience and reach unverified |
| Search and direct | 1,500 | 120 | Useful guide and factual pages; no assumed immediate ranking increase |
| **Total** | **12,500** | **1,000** | These allocations require demonstrated audience reach before they become a forecast |

| Checkpoint | Cumulative net-new unique stored target | Decision |
|---|---:|---|
| 15 October | 100 | Measure conversion after the first 100 relevant visits; verify durable storage and distinct-address counting |
| 22 October | 300 | If below 300, require confirmed creator distribution or revise the forecast; do not invent reach or buy traffic |
| 29 October | 600 | Shift half of remaining drafting effort to the best two sources by actual stored-signup yield |
| 7 November | 1,000 | Report observed total and gap; a missed target stays missed |

These cumulative counts exclude the starting waitlist. Repeated submissions and prior addresses cannot become new signups. Recalculate required visits from observed conversion: `(1,000 minus net-new stored signups) / observed signup rate`; if no reliable rate exists, keep the forecast unknown. Traffic by channel may overlap. Monthly distinct visitor counts cannot be obtained by summing weekly distinct counts.

## Measurement contract

1. Before publication, record production deployment/source SHA, campaign dates, aggregate existing unique stored signup count, fresh traffic export, timezone, visitor definition and filtering. Use the campaign-start timestamp as the new-signup boundary. Never export email addresses into campaign documents.
2. Count a signup only after a durable storage transaction succeeds. Measure normalized unique addresses first seen during the campaign, excluding prior, repeated, test and identified abusive records. A form-success response, provider-accepted email or analytics event alone does not qualify. If durable persistence/counting is unavailable, the signup target is **blocked**.
3. Do not call stored addresses verified, confirmed, unique people, customers or active users. Inbox ownership confirmation would be a separate metric and requires separately authorized email delivery. No nurture sequence, marketing-email consent assumption or outbound messages are included.
4. Keep optional analytics consent intact. Label consenting-only traffic as **consented measured visitors** rather than all humans. Exclude staging, previews, localhost, automated acceptance tests and documented internal/bot traffic. If exclusion evidence is missing, label traffic an unqualified estimate.
5. Use the full links in `post-drafts.md`; campaign ID `cmdtab_waitlist_20261009`. Record source/medium/content only within the actual consent and privacy contract. Never put an address or personal identifier in a URL. If source attribution is unavailable, report source as unknown rather than infer it.
6. Keep visitor counts, engaged visits, pageviews, form responses and stored unique signups separate. Calculate conversion only when numerator and denominator have compatible periods/coverage; consent-limited traffic cannot support an all-signup conversion rate without disclosing the mismatch.
7. At each checkpoint export the same campaign-window aggregate with time zone, exclusions, starting baseline and deployment identity. Fill `weekly-scorecard.csv` with observed numbers only. Keep raw private exports outside public source; report aggregates, not individual addresses.
8. Review search clicks/impressions and AI citations in verified webmaster accounts where available. AI citations and crawler requests are discovery indicators, not human visits or signups. Manual AI-answer checks are small snapshots, not rankings.

## Search and AI discovery principles

Publish practical original guidance with accurate product facts, visible sources, clear availability, and one relevant waitlist CTA. Search and AI discovery changes support distribution but do not guarantee 1,000 waitlist signups in a month. Do not invent credentials, customer reviews, rankings, urgency, or citations. Avoid high-volume near-duplicate pages and prompt-injection text.

Google's [people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) prioritizes useful, trustworthy material. Bing's [Webmaster Guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a) apply to search and AI discovery and prohibit manipulation. Bing's [AI Performance report](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/ai-performance-9f8e7d6c) can show citations in supported experiences; it remains a visibility indicator, separate from human traffic.
