# CmdTab campaign brand guide

## Position

**Tagline:** Choose the exact Mac window, not just the app.

**Campaign promise:** CmdTab is a keyboard-first macOS window switcher that helps people find a specific open window. The public trial is currently in private preview; invite people to request early access and say we will email when access is ready.

**Voice:** Clear, capable, practical, calm. Lead with the work problem and explain one benefit. Avoid inflated claims, urgency tricks, invented customer quotes, competitor attacks, or a promised launch date.

## Visual system

- Near-black: `#05070C`
- Deep navy: `#08111D`
- Cyan accent: `#69D6FF`
- Pale text: `#EAF1FF`
- Typography: SF Pro / system sans; use bold sparingly and generous line spacing.
- Shapes: rounded window cards, restrained glass depth, fine cyan highlights, quiet negative space.
- Official app icon/wordmark: use `/brand/cmdtab.png` from the website. Do not redraw or approximate the icon.

## Asset rules

- `social-visual-01.png` is conceptual artwork showing window selection. It is not product UI or proof of a feature. Give it descriptive alt text: “Illustration of several desktop windows converging on one highlighted window.”
- Use a verified current app recording for feature demos. If using `/showcase` composites, retain their visible “deterministic product composite” disclosure.
- Do not put screenshots, icons, permission prompts, compatibility claims, pricing, or launch dates into generated art.
- Keep small text out of the image; put copy in the caption for accessibility and localization.

## Public facts to verify before publishing

The live `/trial` page says macOS 13.0+, while current repository product facts say macOS 14+. Until the deployed fact is reconciled, omit the minimum macOS version from campaign materials. The download is not currently public; call CmdTab a private preview and link to the waitlist, not to a download.
