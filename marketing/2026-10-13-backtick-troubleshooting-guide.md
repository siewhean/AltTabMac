# Cmd+` Troubleshooting Search Page

## What changed

- Added `/guides/cmd-backtick-not-working-mac` for the concrete search problem “Command+` not working on Mac”.
- Gives a short, ordered diagnosis: frontmost app, active keyboard layout, input-source shortcut conflict, and the distinction between windows and tabs.
- Links Apple’s keyboard-shortcut and window documentation, the existing window-switching guide, and the private-beta form with `utm_content=backtick_guide_cta`.
- Updated the existing switch-between-windows guide’s review date and canonical sitemap date to 2026-10-12 UTC, matching the date of its October CTA edit in UTC.

## Why this experiment

The latest Vercel snapshot showed only 14 pageviews over the prior seven days, with Google the largest identifiable referral source in the latest month. The already scheduled Facebook and Instagram posts had little observed reach and no link clicks. This page targets a specific troubleshooting intent and gives search visitors a useful answer before an optional product link.

## Measurement and limits

- Compare Search Console impressions and clicks for queries containing `cmd backtick`, `command grave`, and `Command+`` over the next 28 days.
- Use the page’s `backtick_guide_cta` UTM to distinguish waitlist visits from other guide traffic where analytics exposes UTM reporting.
- Traffic volume is currently very small. This is a testable content hypothesis, not evidence that the new page will increase traffic or signups.
- No email was sent and no paid promotion was used.
